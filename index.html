Noah Gold

Noah Gold is a filmmaker, writer, and director whose work bridges the philosophical and the emotional, marked by an exploration of existential themes through emotional surrealism and dreamlike natural landscapes.

A self-taught filmmaker, he studied philosophy and physics at the University of Pennsylvania.
